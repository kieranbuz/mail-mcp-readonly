# Privacy policy

**Last updated:** 24 October 2026

mail-mcp-readonly ("the app") is a personal tool, used only by its owner with Google accounts the owner controls.

## What the app accesses

With the owner's consent, the app uses the Gmail read-only permission (`https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly`) to search and read messages, threads, labels and existing drafts. It cannot send, delete, label or modify mail.

## Where data goes

- The app runs on the owner's own computer. It has no server of its own and does not collect data for the developer.
- Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are never published or shared.
- When the owner asks their AI assistant a question about their mail, the relevant message content is passed to that assistant (Anthropic's Claude) under the owner's own account. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
- Content passed to Claude is handled under the owner's Anthropic account terms and settings.
- Data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.
- Data received from Google APIs is not used to develop, improve or train AI or machine-learning models.

## Retention and removal

The app keeps no copy of message content. It may keep a local log of technical activity (for example, which tool ran, when, and whether it succeeded); that log never includes message bodies, subjects, snippets, addresses or search text. The owner's own account addresses may appear in local diagnostic logs on the owner's computer. The owner can remove the app's access to any account at any time from that account's Google settings (myaccount.google.com/connections). Removing access stops the app from reading that account.

## Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including its Limited Use requirements.

## Contact

Use the support email shown on the Google consent screen for this app.
